    Eye accessing cues come from Bandler and Grinder's NLP model and describe a typical right-handed pattern. Controlled studies have not confirmed that eye direction reliably shows recall versus construction, so treat the result as a teaching aid, not a lie detector. Webcam gaze estimation is also approximate; calibrate first and keep the head still.
